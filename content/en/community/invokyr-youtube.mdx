## How to Navigate Invokyr YouTube Playlists

The single biggest mistake new viewers make is starting with the search bar. Search is built to answer a query, not to preserve sequence, so it will happily hand you the middle of a series and call it the top result.

A better routine takes about two minutes:

1. **Open the playlist, not the search results.** A playlist URL keeps the running order intact.
2. **Check who owns it.** Confirm the playlist sits on the channel you actually want, not a re-upload account.
3. **Scan the order before pressing play.** Skim the list so you know how long the run is and where it starts.
4. **Save it to your own library.** A saved playlist survives a cleared browser history and follows you across devices.
5. **Share the playlist link, not a single video.** When you pass it along, the other person gets context instead of a fragment.

## Hardware Is Not the Same as Voice Input

A connected microphone proves your hardware works. It does not prove Invokyr is listening. This is the single most common misunderstanding behind a "mic not working" complaint, and it is why so many players test their microphone in another app, hear themselves fine, and then conclude the game is broken.

The game needs permission to receive spoken input. That permission usually lives in a settings menu, and it can stay off even while your operating system happily routes audio to a perfectly good microphone. Check the function, not the label — menu names shift between builds.

| What you verified | What it actually proves | What to do next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mic works in Discord or a voice recorder | Hardware and system input are fine | Open the in-game voice option and enable it |
| Mic is selected in Windows or macOS | System-level routing is correct | Confirm the game is using that same device |
| A friend finished the ending with a mic | The game supports voice triggers | Compare their enabled settings, not their gear |
| Headset is plugged in and unmuted | Nothing about the game's state | Verify the in-game toggle before retrying |

If you want a reliable baseline for microphone troubleshooting outside the game, [Steam's official support resources](https://help.steampowered.com/en/) cover device detection and voice configuration for players on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## Step-by-Step Fix When Invokyr Mic Not Working

Follow this order whenever the ending refuses to trigger. It keeps progression and troubleshooting separate so you always know which one failed.

1. **Reach the final area.** Progress through the demo until you arrive at the last room. Pause there and confirm this is the intended end area before speaking.
2. **Locate the board game.** If it is not with you, retrace your route. Reaching the room alone is not the solution.
3. **Bring it inside.** Carry or move the board game into the end room and confirm it is genuinely in the room, not at the doorway or in a neighboring space.
4. **Enable voice input.** Open the game's settings and verify the option is active. A connected microphone with voice input disabled will never register the phrase.
5. **Say the game's name.** From inside the room, say "Invokyr" clearly and give the game a moment to respond.
6. **Retry deliberately.** If nothing happens, recheck the board game's position and the voice setting before repeating the phrase.

The dependable order is **room first, board game second, voice input third, phrase last**. That sequence prevents an early voice attempt from being mistaken for a failed ending.

## Common Mistakes That Make the Mic Look Broken

The phrase is only one piece of the puzzle. Players who treat it as the entire solution tend to loop: speak, hear nothing, speak louder, conclude the microphone is faulty. Meanwhile the actual blocker sits untouched in the corner of the room.

Other frequent missteps:

- Leaving the board game behind because the final room is already visible.
- Assuming a system-level microphone test is the same as enabling in-game voice input.
- Repeating the phrase many times in a row instead of verifying setup between attempts.
- Speaking the phrase before entering the room, when the interaction may not be active yet.
- Adding invented steps — item orders, specific standing positions, button prompts — that the confirmed guidance never mentions.

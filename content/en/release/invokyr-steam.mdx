## What Kind of Horror Game Is Invokyr?

Invokyr is a co-op psychological horror game built around a single cursed object: a 1970s board game that hums with energy in the middle of an abandoned house. You aren't exploring a haunted mansion for its own sake — you're trapped there, and the only way out is to finish the game.

The premise is deliberately fairy-tale-adjacent. The Gamemaster, once a beloved toy, now rules the house and speaks to players with a voice that's whimsical and bitter in equal measure. Escape is possible, but only on its terms.

| Element | What It Means for Players |
|---|---|
| Setting | A cold, dark, groaning house with a dusty board game at its center |
| Antagonist | The Gamemaster, a once-beloved toy that now runs the board |
| Objective | Reach the end of the board to win your freedom |
| Failure state | Being dragged into the darkness — though death isn't always the end |
| Tone | Supernatural dread mixed with dark comedy |
| Perspective | First-person, exploration-driven |
| Structure | Run-based, with progression between boards |

That blend of dread and dark comedy is worth highlighting. Steam's own user tags for the game include both "Psychological Horror" and "Dark Comedy," and that tonal mix is a big part of why the demo generated word of mouth. This isn't a game that wants you screaming for ninety minutes straight — it wants you laughing nervously while something with too many limbs walks past the doorway.

## How a Run Works: Dice, Cursed Rules, and the Gamemaster

The core loop revolves around dice. You roll, the board responds, and something new is summoned into the house. Survive what appears, find the tools you need, and push toward the final board.

- **Rolling summons consequences.** Every roll can trigger a monster, a trap, a curse, or an event. The unpredictability is the point.
- **Dice are hidden in the house.** You have to search rooms to recover them, and each die can only be used once, so spending one is a real decision.
- **Special dice bend the rules.** Some heal, some hide you, some hinder whatever is hunting you — but the developers have been explicit that nothing comes without risk.
- **The house is a labyrinth.** Wander too far from the board and you may not find your way back, which turns simple exploration into a navigation problem.
- **Progression carries between boards.** There's a shop, upgrades, equipment, and environmental secrets that gradually reveal more about the mystery.

| System | How It Shapes a Run |
|---|---|
| Cursed rules | Stacking modifiers that make later boards increasingly dangerous |
| Special dice | One-use powers to heal, hide, or hinder hunters |
| Traps and curses | Triggered chaos on a turn-by-turn basis |
| Themed biomes | Jungle, ice mountain, and an oversized toy chest |
| Shop and upgrades | Persistent progression between boards |
| Environmental secrets | Lore fragments about Invokyr and the Gamemaster |

The biome variety is one of the more interesting details to come out of the announcement. A jungle, an ice mountain, and a giant toy chest are not the rooms you'd expect from a haunted-house horror game, and they hint that the "house" is less a physical location and more a space the Gamemaster reshapes at will.

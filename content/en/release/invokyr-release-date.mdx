## What Is Invokyr? A Cursed Board Game You Play With Friends

Invokyr is a first-person, online co-op survival horror game built around a dusty 1970s board game that hums with energy. You and your friends are trapped in an abandoned house ruled by the Gamemaster, a once-beloved toy turned bitter host. Escape is possible, but only if you play — roll the dice, survive whatever the roll summons, and push toward the final board.

Developer notes describe a complete multiplayer loop for 1 to 6 players at Early Access launch, while the store blurb mentions playing with up to five friends. Either way, the game scales from a solo run to a full squad. Death is not always the end, which gives downed players something to do besides spectate.

## Early Access Roadmap: What Changes Before 1.0

Between the Invokyr release date and the eventual 1.0 milestone, the developer expects roughly one year of Early Access, with regular updates themed around new content drops. That timeline is explicitly flexible — Ludogram notes it may change depending on how development and player feedback go.

The 1.0 goal is depth rather than a single headline feature: more boards, more monsters, more events, more dice, more cursed rules, and a deeper narrative around Invokyr and the Game Master. Replayability tuning is also on the list, which makes sense for a game built on randomized rules.

Community involvement is a stated priority. The developer plans to read Steam discussions, Discord chatter, playtests, surveys, and reviews, and to use that input for balancing, difficulty, onboarding, and deciding which monsters, events, and rules players actually enjoy. Player experience from earlier demos and playtests already shaped the current build, according to those same notes.

Invokyr instead makes the rules themselves the threat — the board can change, stack, and punish you mid-run, so the squad's plan is never fully safe.

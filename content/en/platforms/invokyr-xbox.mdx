## What Is Invokyr? A Cursed Board Game You Play With Friends

Invokyr is a first-person co-op psychological horror game wrapped around a 1970s board game that absolutely does not want to be left in the attic. You and up to five friends are trapped in a decaying house with the Gamemaster, a once-beloved toy that now controls the building, the rules, and the things hunting you. Roll the dice, survive whatever gets summoned, and reach the end of the board to earn your freedom — maybe.

The premise is deliberately chaotic. Rules shift, monsters arrive on a roll, and the house itself is a labyrinth of rooms that can swallow you before you ever make it back to the table.
